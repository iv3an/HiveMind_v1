What HiveMind is
and why it exists.

What is HiveMind

An AI panel that generates and stress-tests hackathon ideas so you can start building instead of brainstorming.

HiveMind is a hackathon idea generator powered by four AI agents. You describe your theme, set your team size, and four agents — each with a different lens — propose ideas, debate them, and surface the strongest ones.

It doesn't give you a generic idea list. It gives you battle-tested concepts that have already survived a founder's market check, an engineer's feasibility check, a designer's usability check, and a critic's reality check.

Why we built it

Because the first two hours of a hackathon are the most wasted.

Teams spend the opening stretch arguing over ideas instead of building. Someone proposes an idea, someone else says "that already exists", and you end up picking whatever seemed least controversial — not what's actually winnable.

HiveMind front-loads that debate. In 60 seconds you get the argument that would have taken two hours, plus a clear recommendation on what to build and why.

How the agents work

Four agents, one pipeline.

The first three agents run in parallel, each from their own perspective. The Devil's Advocate runs last, armed with all three outputs, and forces the ideas to be sharper.

🚀 Startup Founder

Evaluates and generates ideas through a business lens. Asks: is there a real market? Can this make money? Are people actually in pain here? Focuses on monetization paths and market size over pure cleverness.

⚙️ Hacker Engineer

Generates technically impressive ideas that are realistically scoped to 48 hours. Specifies the exact APIs, models, and stack — not vague "use AI" suggestions. Focuses on what will wow technical judges while still being shippable.

🎨 UX/Product Thinker

Designs ideas around specific users with specific pain points. Asks: when does the user feel the value? Can a stranger understand this in 30 seconds? Focuses on the demo moment and emotional resonance — the things that win audience prizes.

😈 Devil's Advocate

Reviews all three agents' outputs and challenges every idea. Calls out what already exists by name, identifies the fatal flaw, and forces specific improvements. Then picks the top 3 ideas that are most likely to win despite the weaknesses.

The Stack

Minimal. No databases, no accounts, no tracking.

Everything runs in memory for the duration of your session. No data is stored between sessions. Your ideas stay yours.
