The Team
Meet the agents

Each agent has one role. Together they cover everything that matters at a hackathon.

Researcher

Scans the landscape for real market gaps, existing solutions, and opportunity spaces relevant to your theme.

Ideator

Generates concrete, buildable ideas from the research. Focuses on novelty, user value, and hackathon fit.

Engineer

Names the exact stack — APIs, models, architecture. Answers: can this actually be built in 48 hours?

Critic

Challenges every idea. Finds what already exists, the fatal flaw, and forces the best ones to get stronger.
